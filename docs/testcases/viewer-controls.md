# Test cases: viewer control bar

Course used: `viewer.html?course=aeris-3-laps-lake` (19.9 km, 43 turns, sections Lap 1 of 3, Lap 2 of 3, Lap 3 of 3, then Lake loop). "Phone" means a window of 800 px wide or less, or 480 px high or less in landscape; "desktop" is anything wider than 800 px. The six controls are `#prevTurn`, `#prev`, `#play`, `#next`, `#nextTurn` and `#more`. In the console, `__viewer.getIndex()` and `__viewer.setIndex(i)` read and set the position, and `__viewer.points.length - 1` is the last point.

| ID | Steps | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| VC-01 order | At 1280x800 and at 390x844 read the buttons left to right. | Previous turn, Back, Play, Next, Next turn, and on the phone More last. The order is the same on both. |
| VC-02 no turns | Open a course with no turns (or set `hidden` on `#prevTurn` and `#nextTurn` in the console). | The two turn buttons are hidden; Back, Play and Next keep their order. |
| VC-03 icons | Look at each button, and inspect `#bar` and `#back`. | Every icon is an inline `<svg>` (no text arrows, no emoji). Back and Next are single triangles; Prev turn and Next turn are a triangle with a bar, so they look different from Back and Next; Play is a triangle, Pause two bars, Replay a circular arrow; More is three dots; the back link is a chevron. |
| VC-04 desktop labels | At 1280x800. | Each button shows icon and word: `Prev turn`, `Back`, `Play`, `Next`, `Next turn`. Icon is left of the word for Prev turn, Back and Play, and right of the word for Next and Next turn. The back link reads chevron plus `All courses`. More is not shown. |
| VC-05 phone labels | At 390x844. | Each button shows the icon with a caption of about 11 px under it, using the same words as desktop, plus `More`. The back link is the chevron alone. |
| VC-06 phone size | At 390x844 and 360x640, measure each dock button (`getBoundingClientRect`) and the back link. | Every button is at least 44 px wide and 48 px tall; the back link is 44x44. No caption is cut off, and `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= window.innerWidth`. |
| VC-07 landscape phone | At 844x390. | The dock is one row; captions are hidden (icons only); each button is at least 44 px tall and 44 px wide. |
| VC-08 labels for assistive tech | Read `aria-label` and `title` of each button. | Each names its action, and the title keeps the key: `Previous turn (Down arrow)`, `Back one step (Left arrow)`, `Next step (Right arrow)`, `Next turn (Up arrow)`. The icons are `aria-hidden="true"`. |
| VC-09 Play is primary | Look at Play on desktop and phone. | Red fill (`--accent`) with white icon and text on both layouts. |
| VC-10 Play states | Press Play; press it again; then `__viewer.setIndex(__viewer.points.length - 1)`. | While playing it shows the pause icon, `Pause`, and its `aria-label` and `title` say Pause. Stopped it shows the play icon and `Play`. At the last point, stopped, it shows the replay icon and `Replay`, with `aria-label` "Replay from the start". Pressing it then restarts from the first point and shows Pause. |
| VC-11 disabled at the start | `__viewer.setIndex(0)`. | Back and Prev turn have the `disabled` attribute, look faded (about 40% opacity), show a not-allowed cursor on desktop and do not change on hover or press. Next, Next turn and Play are enabled. |
| VC-12 disabled at the end | `__viewer.setIndex(__viewer.points.length - 1)`. | Next and Next turn are disabled, Back and Prev turn are enabled. |
| VC-13 turn buttons follow the turns | Go to a point just after the last turn's junction, then press Next turn until nothing is left. Do the same going back. | Next turn becomes disabled exactly when pressing it would do nothing (no later turn), and Prev turn exactly when there is no earlier turn. Up and Down arrows act the same way. |
| VC-14 keyboard after disabling | Tab to Next, press Enter until it becomes disabled at the last point. Then press the Left arrow and Space. | Focus moves to Play (never lost). Left steps back one point and Space toggles playback; the shortcuts keep working. |
| VC-15 hover, pressed, focus | On desktop hover, press and Tab to each button and the speed select. | Enabled buttons get a grey hover and a darker pressed state (Play gets a darker red). A 3 px blue (`#1e6fd9`) outline shows on keyboard focus only, not on mouse click. |
| VC-16 More pressed | On a phone tap More; tap it again. | While the sheet is open (`aria-expanded="true"`) the button has a dark filled background; closed it is back to white. |
| VC-17 current section | Open More on a phone (or look at the bar on desktop). Jump to Lap 2, then play past into Lap 3. | The section button for the current section has `aria-current="true"` and a visibly selected style (pale amber fill, amber border, bold); only one has it, and it moves when the section changes. |
| VC-18 reduced motion | Turn on "reduce motion" in the OS and press a button. | The button changes colour at once, with no transition and no downward shift. |
| VC-19 unchanged | Arrow keys, Space, the slider, the speed select, the turn banner, Where am I, the outline and `__viewer.lineCheck()` (below 1 m). | All behave as before. |
| VC-20 site check | Run `node tools/check_site.mjs`. | Prints `SITE OK`. It fails if one of the six ids is missing, or if the `#bar` block or the `#back` link contains one of the characters `◀ ▶ ⏮ ⏭ ⋯ ←` or the entities `&#9664; &#9654; &#9198; &#9197; &#8943; &larr;`. |
