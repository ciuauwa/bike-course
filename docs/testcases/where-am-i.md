# Test cases: Where am I

Course used: `viewer.html?course=aeris-3-laps-lake` (19.9 km, 3 laps then the lake loop). Open the browser console for the `__viewer.locate` calls. `setFix(lat, lon, accuracyM)` turns Where am I on without asking the browser for your location and handles the point exactly like a real fix. "Info box" is `#locInfo`: a box at the bottom left of the map on desktop, a strip across the bottom on phone layouts. Road routes come from `https://brouter.de/brouter` (needs internet). `__viewer.locate.getState()` returns `{ on, distM, alongM, roadM, targetAlongM, routePoints, message, requests }`.

| ID | Steps | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| WA-01 on route | `__viewer.locate.setFix(-6.334505, 106.633787, 10)` | Button shows pressed (`aria-pressed="true"`). A green dot with a white border and a faint green circle appear. No dashed line. Info box: "You are on the route, at km X.XX of 19.90." The map zooms to the point once. |
| WA-02 200 m off route | `__viewer.locate.setFix(-6.343404, 106.622585, 10)` (200 m north of a route vertex; another stretch of the route is nearer, about 140 m to the west) | A green dashed line joins the dot to the nearest route point while the road route loads, with the message "You are 140 m from the route. Head west to rejoin it at km X.XX." About 1 s later (internet on): the dashed line is replaced by a solid green line (white outline) along roads to a small green ring on the route, and `getState().roadM` is a number and `routePoints` above 1. Message: "You are off the route. Follow the green line for N m to rejoin it at km X.XX." (N is a multiple of 10 below 1000, otherwise "N.N km"). The map fits the route once (not above zoom 17), with the route clear of the legend, the info box and the button. `getState().requests` is 2. |
| WA-03 beyond 20 km | `__viewer.locate.setFix(-6.2, 106.8, 10)` | Info box: "You are 23.1 km from this course." No line is drawn, "Next turn from here" is hidden, `getState().requests` does not increase, `roadM` is null and `routePoints` is 0. A fix between 30 m and 20 km away does route. |
| WA-04 low accuracy | `__viewer.locate.setFix(-6.334505, 106.633787, 150)` | Info box ends with " GPS ±150 m." The accuracy circle has a 150 m radius. With accuracy 100 or less the sentence is absent. |
| WA-05 no re-fit | After WA-01, pan or zoom the map, then `__viewer.locate.setFix(-6.3346, 106.6338, 10)` | The dot moves and the text updates. The map does not move or zoom. |
| WA-06 lap ambiguity | Run `__viewer.setIndex(0)`, then `__viewer.locate.nearest(-6.3345, 106.6340).alongM`; then `__viewer.setIndex(175)` (about km 7.0) and call `nearest` again with the same point | First call: `alongM` below 3400 (lap 1). Second call: `alongM` at or above 6950 (a later lap). The info box on `setFix` with the same point shows the matching km. |
| WA-07 jump button | After WA-02 or WA-06 press "Next turn from here" | Viewer moves to the view point nearest the road route's target km (the km in the message), or to the rider's nearest point when no road route is shown (slider, status bar and Street View change); the turn banner shows the next turn after that point, and the map pans there. The dot and info box stay (locating does not stop). |
| WA-08 turning off | With Where am I on, tap the button again, tap the x (`#locClose`) in the info box, or run `__viewer.locate.stop()` | All three do the same: `aria-pressed="false"`, dot, circle, dashed line, green road route and ring, and info box are gone. `__viewer.locate.isOn()` is `false`. A route answer that arrives after this is ignored (nothing is drawn, `getState().routePoints` stays 0). |
| WA-09 waiting for a fix | Tap Where am I on a device and allow location | Info box says "Finding your location…" until the first fix, then switches to the route text. |
| WA-10 permission denied | Block location for the site, tap Where am I | Info box: "Location is blocked. Allow location for this site in your browser settings, then tap Where am I again." Button returns to off. Tapping again tries again. |
| WA-11 unavailable or timeout | Turn GPS off or use a place with no signal, tap Where am I | Info box: "Could not get your location. Check that GPS is on and try again." Button returns to off. |
| WA-12 not supported | Open over plain `http://` on a non-local host, or in a browser without geolocation | Info box: "This browser cannot share your location here." Button stays off. |
| WA-13 what is sent and stored | Use Where am I off the route, then check Application storage and the Network tab | No `localStorage` key holds a position (the existing `streetview.index.<slug>` key holds only the view-point index). The only requests carrying coordinates go to `brouter.de/brouter` (two per batch), and none while on the route or beyond 20 km. The note under the Street View (More sheet on phones) reads "Where am I sends your position to brouter.de to find the road back." |
| WA-14 phone widths 360x640 | Open at 360x640 and at 390x844, press Where am I (and the Stops toggle), run WA-02 with the longest message (add accuracy 150: `setFix(-6.343404, 106.622585, 150)`) | The button is an icon only (crosshair), exactly 44x44 px, with `aria-label` and `title` "Where am I", about 9 px under the zoom control and with the same right inset; it overlaps neither the zoom control, the legend row nor the attribution. Off: white with a dark icon; on: green with a white icon. The info strip spans the map width (8 px each side), sits just above the attribution, is at most 96 px tall, text at most 3 lines (scrolls inside if longer), "Next turn from here" and the x (44x44) on its right. No horizontal scroll on the page. |
| WA-15 landscape phone 844x390 | Open at 844x390, run WA-02 | Same as WA-14: no overlap, no horizontal scroll, the info box scrolls inside itself if the map is short. |
| WA-16 desktop 1280x800 | Open at 1280x800, run WA-02 | Same as WA-14 except: the button is 34x34 px like the zoom buttons, and the info box is a box at the bottom left, at most 360 px wide, with the text above the buttons. The turn banner and legend are unchanged. |
| WA-17 nothing else changed | Run `__viewer.lineCheck()` and use Play, Next turn, the More sheet and the Stops list | `maxDevM` is below 1. All controls behave as before. |
| WA-18 road route fallback | Go offline (or block `brouter.de` in devtools), then `__viewer.locate.setFix(-6.343404, 106.622585, 10)` | The dashed straight line and the compass message stay, with " (road route unavailable)" added after at most 10 s. `roadM` is null and `routePoints` is 0. No further request goes out until the throttle (WA-19) allows. |
| WA-19 throttle | With a road route shown (WA-02), call `setFix` again with a point 10 m away, then with a point 100 m away within 15 s, then with a point 100 m from the first one after 15 s | `getState().requests` stays at 2 for the first two calls and only goes up (by 2) on the last. Never more than one batch is in flight: a `setFix` while the first is loading makes no request. |
| WA-20 stale answers | Run WA-02 and, before the route arrives (throttle devtools network to Slow 3G), call `__viewer.locate.setFix(-6.334505, 106.633787, 10)` (on the route) or `stop()` | The late route is not drawn: the message says "You are on the route ..." (or the info box is gone) and `routePoints` is 0. |
| WA-21 closer ahead | Pick a point where the road back to a course point 400 m further along is shorter than to the nearest one | The solid line ends at the ring 400 m further along, and the km in the message is about 0.4 above `getState().alongM / 1000`; `getState().targetAlongM` is that further point. |
| WA-22 waiting pulse | Tap the button on a device (before the first fix), or run WA-02 with the network throttled | The button pulses softly until the fix or the first road route arrives. With the OS setting "reduce motion" on, it does not animate. |
