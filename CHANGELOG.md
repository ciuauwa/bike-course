# Changelog

- 2026-11-26: New course, CitraGarden to Gunung Dago (28.3 km, 287 m climb): out and back from the CitraGarden Serpong roundabout to Wisata Alam Gunung Dago in Parung Panjang, through the ticket gate and up the steep park road to the top (163 m).
- 2026-11-26: The route check accepts real road features: `hairpins` in course.json allows a mountain hairpin that would read as a tiny loop, `uTurns` allows a U-turn at a gap in the median of a divided road (worded "U-turn at the gap in the median"), and stale declarations warn. `added` may now carry a time (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`) so two courses added on the same day sort correctly.
- 2026-11-26: The Street View viewer's controls are one system on phone and desktop: the same SVG icons and the same order (previous turn, back, play, next, next turn), the same words (icon over a caption on phones), Play is the primary button, Back and Next turn grey out when there is nowhere to go, Play becomes Pause and Replay, More looks pressed while open, and the current section is highlighted.
- 2026-11-26: Each course card now shows an elevation profile chart, the highest point, the steepest gradient (over 100 m, uphill only) and the share of the route on main roads. Rebuild a course with tools/make_course.py to get the new figures.
- 2026-11-26: The climb figure is now computed from the course's elevation profile instead of BRouter's smoothed estimate, so it is closer to what Garmin Connect shows, and each course card also shows the unpaved share of the route.
- 2026-11-26: New course, CitraGarden Lap + Situ Cisawang (31.6 km, 110 m climb): one end-to-end lap of Jalan CitraGarden Serpong, then a loop out through Kampung Ekowisata Keranggan to Situ Cisawang and home past Danau Suradita and Danau Abah.
- 2026-11-26: The course list now shows the newest course first. Each course.json can set `added` (`YYYY-MM-DD`); courses without it come last, ordered by name. The viewer's default course is now the newest one.
- 2026-11-25: New course, CitraGarden 3 Laps + Danau Abah (18.5 km), starting and finishing at the same roundabout as the Aeris course: three end-to-end laps of Jalan CitraGarden Serpong between the Kuburan Cina turning circle and the Patung Kuda gate, then out to Danau Abah and back.
- 2026-11-22: In the Street View viewer, tapping the turn banner now goes to the turn it shows, and the header status line (section, km, turn counter) opens a Course outline sheet listing every turn by lap, where tapping a row jumps to that turn.
- 2026-11-22: Where am I now draws a road route back to the course (from the public BRouter service, straight dashed line as fallback), is an icon button, and shows as a compact strip with a close button on phones. Your position is sent to brouter.de only while you are off the route.
- 2026-11-22: Street View viewer has a Where am I button on the map: it shows your position, how far you are from the route and which way to head to rejoin it, and can jump the viewer to your next turn. The position stays in the browser.
- 2026-11-22: Route checker (tools/check_route.py) catches unintended spurs, tiny loops and stray U-turns, and make_course.py refuses to write a course that fails it. Fixed the stray 44 m dead-end detour at the Lapangan stop (course is now 19.9 km), and the blue ridden line on the map now follows the road instead of cutting corners.
- 2026-11-20: Course list cards show a route map thumbnail, distance, climb, ride time, turns, surface, difficulty, route breakdown and stops.
- 2026-11-20: Favicon and link-preview (WhatsApp) title, description and image. Regenerate the images with tools/make_share_assets.py (needs Pillow).
- 2026-11-20: On phones the map is now the larger pane and the Street View the smaller one.
- 2026-11-20: Stops on the map are numbered, and a Stops list in the map legend names each one.
- 2026-11-20: Phone layout for the Street View viewer: controls in a bottom dock, turn instruction as a strip under the image, extras behind a More button, and a ridden / ahead legend on the map.
- 2026-11-20: Added Warung Bu Endi Jabon as a rest stop on the Aeris lake loop (course is now 19.9 km).
